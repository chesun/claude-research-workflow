# Lean card calibration — practice cards for the owner (plan 2026-10-12, Task 7)

Three short fidelity cards in the format of `templates/theory/fidelity-card.md`. Some may hold a Lean statement that does **not** say what the paper says; some may be faithful. You are not told how many. The answers are sealed outside the repository until you finish.

**For each card (about 5–10 minutes each):**
1. Read the paper statement (section 1, left column).
2. Use the glossary (section 2) and the blind back-translation (section 8) to work out what the Lean statement says.
3. Fill in section 3, the per-part map: is the Lean version *equal*, *weaker*, *stronger* or *missing* something?
4. Fill in section 9 with one concrete instance: pick numbers, then compute what the paper says and what the Lean statement says.
5. Write any notes in section 10, then set **Verdict** (FAITHFUL / WITH-NOTED-DIFFERENCES / UNFAITHFUL) and **Signed off** (your initials and the date).

Note the time you spend on each. When you are done, tell Claude. The answers are then unsealed, and the result (caught, missed, false alarm, time) goes in the session-log read-out. It anchors spec acceptance #8.

These cards are practice. Their statements are toy versions; none of them is a blueprint node.
