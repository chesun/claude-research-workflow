# ADR-0005 Review — fit-for-purpose
**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Reviewer:** fit-for-purpose critic (independent, general-purpose agent)
**Target:** decisions/0005_lean-sign-off-checklist-and-independent-audit.md
**Score:** 66/100
**Status:** Active

## Verdict

ADR-0005 responds to the right pilot evidence with the right shape of fix: the checklist matches how the owner actually worked (session log §12, "the pilot's working form is the numbered checklist"), dropping the status-line approval removes a ritual the owner correctly called redundant on the happy path, and adding an independent auditor is a genuine third reader. But as written it does not keep its own "Rules out" promise. The signature binds to nothing: the card has no content hash, the validator checks no dates, and once the second approval is gone there is no human touchpoint left between a post-signature card edit and `verified` — Claude both verifies the signature and appends the actor-`user` line. The "a mistake passes only if all three miss it" claim is wrong for the omission class (the owner only ever sees the back-translator's list), the new symbol-table input leaks the formalizer's mapping into both supposedly independent readers while silently discarding the pilot's independent-glossary cross-check, and the `Independent audit:` field is a bare header value with no artifact — below the repo's own evidence-gating bar. The "Changes to make" list omits the mechanical checks that would make the decision enforceable. The direction is sound; the ADR needs a hardening pass before Decided.

## What is right (for the record, per the ADR's own justify-don't-narrate rule)

- Decisions 1 and 2 follow the pilot evidence directly (§12: the checklist is what worked; the second approval repeated the signature) and Decision 1's SHA-binding of `verified` to the same statement hash is correctly stated.
- The auditor's blindness choices (no draft card, no back-translation, no verdicts) are well aimed at the formalizer's framing, and the disagreement-stop with owner authority preserves the human as final arbiter.
- "Calibration before trust" for the auditor, the withdrawal path, and the Not-decided items (second auditor on disagreement; retroactive audit defaulting to run) are the right open questions.
