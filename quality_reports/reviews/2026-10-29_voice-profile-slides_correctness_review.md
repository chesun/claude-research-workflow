## Verdict

The change is coherent where it matters most: the papers-vs-slides split is stated identically in ADR-0007 item 3, the reference (`voice-profile.md:19-24`), `storyteller.md:63` ("Never use the paper profile"), and `humanize/SKILL.md:58`; a repo-wide grep found no surviving "papers only" or "slides never get it" text. Tool grants check out — `storyteller` has Bash (`storyteller.md:5`) and resolves the profile itself, `storyteller-critic` has none (`storyteller-critic.md:5`) and `/talk` (which has Bash, `talk/SKILL.md:5`) resolves and passes the path (`talk/SKILL.md:35`), matching `voice-profile.md:25-27`. ADR-0007 carries all four required fields and follows the ADR-0004/0005 partial-supersession precedent exactly (`0007:7` mirrors `0005:7`; `0006:4` mirrors `0004:4`), and the only edit to ADR-0006 is its Status line. The defects are in the plumbing details: the reference's one "how to resolve" instruction still joins `profile.md` only, so the dispatcher and agents that defer to it would resolve the wrong file for slides; the gitignore statements in two files omit PowerPoint while the build's step 7 and ADR-0007 include it; and the `.pptx` extraction recipe, while pointing at the right XML (`<a:t>` runs in `ppt/slides/slideN.xml`), as written loses the bullet boundaries and title identity that the slide schema's Titles/Bullets sections and the step-4 rhythm metrics (bullets per slide, words per bullet) need. None of these blocks the design; all three should be fixed before the first `--slides` build.
