## Verdict

v3 is **fit, efficient, and worth doing** — it correctly absorbed the prior review (62/100), collapsed four agents to one voice agent plus a 10-minute diff, decoupled the modernize overlay-mirror, and stayed disciplined (the harmonization adds design *depth* to the one surviving workstream, not new scope). The core diagnosis is **accurate and verified against the repo**: `/humanize` is intact (SKILL.md present; anti-ai-prose.md:165 "No hooks"), `anti-ai-prose.md` is a subtractive ~35-pattern / 6-category catalog (anti-ai-prose.md:64), and both `anti-ai-prose.md` (§ Voice profiles, lines 46–58) *and* `humanize/SKILL.md` (§ Voice profile inference, line 28) already use "voice profile" for a document-type **register** knob — so the naming collision the plan wants to fix is real, and the rename to "register profile" is well-motivated. The subtractive-vs-additive framing is correct. **The harmonization is mostly sound and buildable, but it over-commits to two design choices the survey is supposed to *validate*, not assume:** (1) folding the additive voice pass into `/humanize` as a "combined operation" contradicts both the upstream's deliberate two-skill separation *and* this repo's own `/humanize` idempotency contract (SKILL.md:90); and (2) the "profile wins for its scope" supersession rule is under-bounded and could wholesale-legitimize genuine AI tells that happen to appear in the exemplar corpus. Both are fixable by reframing them as open questions for the survey. The plan is ready to run with that one reframing.

## What the harmonization gets right

- **The naming collision is real and the rename is correct.** Verified in two places (`anti-ai-prose.md` § Voice profiles; `humanize/SKILL.md` § Voice profile inference) — both use "voice profile" for a path-inferred *register* knob. Reserving "voice profile" for the positive per-paradigm spec and renaming the existing one "register profile" is the right fix and should be done before the term spreads further.
- **Subtractive vs additive is an accurate framing.** `anti-ai-prose`/`/humanize` strip ~35 tells (subtractive); a voice spec is a positive target (additive). They genuinely act in opposite directions on the same text, so composing them is coherent.
- **The central premise is validated by an independent web check.** Upstream `/voice-profile` exists and does exactly what the plan assumes ("extracts your written voice from prior papers, then audits drafts against it — the positive counterpart to `/humanize`"). There is a real, buildable exemplar to adapt.
- **Survey-only / build-deferred posture is right** (line 43: "This survey does not build"), and the clean survey→decide→build separation matches the ADR-0002 review gate ethos.
- **Discipline held — no scope creep in the survey itself.** v3 cut workstreams B and D, decoupled the overlay mirror, and kept one agent + a diff. The harmonization adds design *specification* to the surviving workstream, not new workstreams; the bias the prior review flagged (shrink → accretion) does not recur here.

## What it misses or should sharpen

- **It states design conclusions as settled where they should be survey hypotheses.** The plan says (line 25) the survey "must validate the following harmonization," then presents "closes the carve-out TODO," "`/humanize` becomes the combined operation," and "profile wins for its scope" as decided. Two of those (combined operation; unbounded supersession) are likely *wrong* on the evidence above. Reframe them as candidates the survey tests and can reject.
- **Bigger build than the survey implies.** The eventual build now spans a rename touching `anti-ai-prose.md` + `humanize/SKILL.md` + `writer.md` + `writer-critic.md`, plus a new additive skill/scoring path, plus storyteller-critic carve-outs. Cost isn't a constraint, but the survey should *size* this honestly so the follow-on plan isn't a surprise.
- **No adherence/evaluation loop.** Nothing specifies how you'd confirm a generated draft actually matches the profile (the additive analogue of `/humanize`'s idempotency check). This is the single most important thing an additive voice tool needs and is currently absent.
- **Storyteller path unaddressed.** The carve-out TODO explicitly names `storyteller-critic`; the voice-profile design is paper-only. Either scope slides in or note explicitly that slide carve-outs are handled at the register/catalog level.

## Sharpest single recommendation

Reframe the three pre-decided design claims (carve-out closure, combined `/humanize`, "profile wins") as **open questions the survey must resolve**, and steer the answers toward: (a) keep `/humanize` subtractive and add a *separate* upstream-style voice skill for the additive direction — folding both into one pass breaks idempotency (SKILL.md:90) and diverges from the upstream's deliberate two-skill split; (b) put the register-general academic carve-outs at the **catalog/register level** (unconditional, covering storyteller-critic) per the TODO's own text, with voice-profile supersession as a bounded, *enumerated* additional layer that cannot disable Critical tells wholesale; and (c) name "how conformance is measured" as an explicit deliverable. With that reframing the plan is **ready to run** — it's one focused agent closing two real TODOs, and the method is sound.
