## Verdict

The change is sound in substance and lean in size: the rename, the three field-convention carve-outs and the author-voice pointer each match the settled design (ADR-0006 items 4–6; `quality_reports/reviews/2026-09-22_infra-survey_C-voice.md` Q3) and nothing added contradicts the catalog rows R1, L3 or L4 — the R1 carve-out spares only `Importantly,`/`Notably,` (not in R1's example list) while reaffirming R1's own examples as filler; the L3 carve-out is consistent with the row's existing "when 'do' works" qualifier and is mirrored verbatim in writer-critic's McCloskey item 8; L4 appears in no critic deduction table, so the carve-out binds only the humanizer. The register scoping ("`academic` and `slide`") matches how both critics and `/humanize` name and infer the profiles, the frontmatter path scope (`paper/**`, `talks/**`) covers where the conventions are needed, and the reference file depends on the rule's paragraph ("beyond the register carve-out", voice-profile.md:106–107) rather than duplicating it — the single-home discipline holds. The diff is +9/−2 lines, +854 bytes (12,524 → 13,378, +6.8%), on a path-scoped rule with no always-loaded budget impact. What keeps the score below 90 is an incomplete rename ripple: the plan's update list (plan:82) omitted `storyteller.md`, which still tells the storyteller to "pass against the `slide` voice profile" — exactly the term collision the rename exists to remove — and the living docs page `upstream-differences.md` still describes the registers as "voice profiles". Everything else is recommendation-grade.
