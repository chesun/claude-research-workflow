## Verdict

The plan is already substantially leaner than the survey design it builds from — one profile instead of two, no Class B overlay content, user-scope storage (`~/.claude/voice/`) that keeps the filled profile out of every repo and off every sync path, and all new reference material lazy-loaded. I verified the budget claims: `anti-ai-prose.md` is path-scoped (frontmatter `paths:`, lines 1–10), `.claude/references/` is lazy, and the only always-loaded growth is the CLAUDE.md header field + skills row (~0.2k on a 9,790-char CLAUDE.md, against ~46.6k headroom under the 150k cap per CHANGELOG.md:37) — immaterial. The `pdftotext` dependency the plan hedges on is in fact present (TeX Live 2026, poppler via winget, and Git's ucrt64 all ship it on this machine). The register carve-outs (deliverable 5) close a standing backlog item (TODO.md:43) with no corpus and are the best value-per-byte in the plan. The over-build is concentrated in one place: `bin/voice_features.py` in v1 — a gated code deliverable costing two independent reviews (ADR-0002; independent-review.md:12) plus a test file plus downstream sync, whose output nothing consumes as a gate and whose rhythm numbers, on a corpus of one paper, an agent can estimate for an advisory profile. Secondary costs: the carve-out/floor row lists are slated to appear in at least three places, the sync scope is mislabeled (ADR-0006 and CLAUDE.md are not Class A — file-classes.toml:147, :153–154), and two small YAGNI items (the style-models machinery ahead of any input paper; the CLAUDE.md override field with no current user). Deferring the feature script and tightening the sync list makes the build two reviews, three files, and roughly a third cheaper for the same v1 outcome.

5. Defer the style-models template and `--model` flag until the first style-model paper is supplied; drop (or consciously keep) the CLAUDE.md override field.
