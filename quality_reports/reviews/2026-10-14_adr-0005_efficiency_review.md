# ADR-0005 Review — efficiency
**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Reviewer:** efficiency critic (independent, general-purpose agent)
**Target:** decisions/0005_lean-sign-off-checklist-and-independent-audit.md
**Score:** 82/100
**Status:** Active

## Verdict

ADR-0005 is close to the leanest design that meets its goal, and its two structural moves are well grounded in pilot evidence: dropping the second approval removes an owner action the pilot showed was pure repetition (the validator already re-checks every `formalized`/`verified` evidence line against the signed card and PASS report — `bin/blueprint_check.py:597–618` — and the pilot's negative control FAILed as it should, session log §12), and the checklist-first card codifies the form that actually worked (owner finished in ~30 min only once Claude translated each part into numbered lines, §12 "Acceptance #8"). Owner cost per node falls from ~30 min + two approvals to ~30 min or less with no approvals; agent cost rises from 3 dispatches per node (formalizer ×2 + back-translator) to 4, where the marginal fable dispatch is trivial next to the formalizer's 101k-token prove stage (§12 cost table). The auditor neither duplicates nor can replace the back-translator — the back-translator's value is precisely that it cannot see the paper, the auditor's that it can — and merging them would destroy the blindness that makes the checklist trustworthy. The real defects are second-order: the new shared symbol table silently removes the pilot's independent-glossary cross-check and becomes a single point of failure for all three readers; the retroactive audit is filed as "Not decided" when the validator change makes it effectively mandatory; and the restructured card keeps two sections (5 and 8) that the checklist now duplicates, written by agents whose output nobody in the flow reads any more.

## Cost accounting (probe answers in numbers)

- **Owner minutes per node:** pilot ≈ 30 min (card, interactive) + 2 status-line approvals + withdrawal option; new design ≈ ≤ 30 min (checklist pre-written) + 0 approvals + occasional disagreement adjudication. Calibration (30+ min) was one-off and is done; the auditor's calibration costs the owner nothing.
- **Dispatches per node:** pilot 3 (formalizer formalize, back-translator, formalizer prove); new 4 (+ auditor, fable). One-off: auditor calibration (3 cards). Retro audit of the pilot node: 1 dispatch, forced by finding 2.
- **Auditor vs back-translator:** complements, not duplicates. The back-translator is blind to the paper (checklist cannot be bent toward it); the auditor reads the paper (does the comparison the owner is a thin filter for). Merging or substituting either direction loses the property that justifies the other. One fable dispatch is the cheapest element of the whole lane (§12 table: the prove stage alone was 101k tokens).
