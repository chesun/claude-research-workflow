## Verdict

The redesign does what the round-1 reviews asked and what the docs now say. Every log line `repoint` writes validates: the direct demotion (`<proved+> -> survived-search | blueprint-tools | <retired chain files> | stmt:`) and the cascade line (`<checked+> -> proved | blueprint-tools | <cause node file> | stmt: proof:<current hash>`) both parse under `LOG_RE`, classify as `down`, and carry an existing in-repo locator, so `--apply` ends at PASS on every shape probed (two-level and three-level chains, a `formalized` and a `verified` node, a definition superseded by a definition, a `conditional` predecessor, names sorted against dependency order). The climb-back was traced end to end against the validator's `-> checked` rule: after the demoted foundation is re-proved and re-checked, a cascaded node's `proved -> checked` line with its **original** two verifier reports is accepted (the cascade is a `down` move, so `last_proved_hash` is still the original `-> proved` hash and the reports still name that hash), and a wrong-hash line is rejected; `verify-input` refuses the cascaded node until the foundation is checked and accepts it afterwards. `verify-input`'s readiness rules match the skill's step 1 and the validator (`_ready` equals the `checked` dependency rule, including `imported`), its `**Target:**`/`**Proof:**` lines are what `_report_problem` reads, and the two extra sections match `proof-verifier.md` item 3 by name and meaning. All three suites pass (119 / 54 / 60) and `agent_lint` reports PASS on the repo's 38 files. Every round-1 finding is fixed and, with the exceptions listed under finding 3, pinned by a test. Two things are wrong rather than weak: the per-key continuation tracking added to fix round-1's empty-description finding crashes the whole lint run (uncaught `IndexError`, no verdict printed) on a valid YAML shape, `description:` with its text on the next indented line; and the cascade is not limited to nodes affected by the repoint, so a dry run on one retired node also demotes any `checked` node in the blueprint that already rested on an unchecked claim, with a cause this repoint never touched, which neither the docstring nor the three docs describe.

Probes ran in the session scratchpad against a copy of `bin/` (`claude-r2/bin`), with the test file's own fixture helpers and sentinel labels only; mutations ran on further copies. Nothing in the repo was modified except this report.
