## Verdict

The decision is close to minimal in footprint: the companion diff is +28/−12 lines across six on-demand files, nothing in `.claude/rules/` or `CLAUDE.md` changed (verified by grep; the only rule that names the voice profile, `anti-ai-prose.md:65,182`, is untouched), dropping `avoid_vocab` removes work rather than adding it, and the ADR's 40 lines sit between ADR-0006 (47) and ADR-0007 (38). The waste is in the build procedure and in where text lives, not in the size of the change. Each style model is now read twice by two subagents (one for the three structure sections, one for `## Argument`); the skill never says that existing notes may be reused, so a schema-only rebuild re-dispatches every per-document subagent; section counts are hard-coded in three files, and this very diff had to touch two of them just to bump a number; the reference, meant to be the schema's single home, states the same seven-item content list three times; and the ADR's Context paragraph carries analysis that the decision-log rule says belongs in a session log, while the session-log section it cites does not yet contain those decisions. None of this blocks the decision; all of it is cheap to fix before commit.
