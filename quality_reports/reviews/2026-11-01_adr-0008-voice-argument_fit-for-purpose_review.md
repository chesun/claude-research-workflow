## Verdict

The ADR does what the owner asked. An `## Argument` section now heads `profile.md` and `style-models.md` (`voice-profile.md:69`, `:129`), precedence is split by level (ADR-0008:24; `voice-profile.md:116`), `writer` plans from `## Argument` first (`writer.md:104`), `writer-critic` checks *model wins* points against targets the profile states so it needs no shell (`writer-critic.md:234`), and `avoid_vocab` is gone from the schema (`voice-profile.md:63–66`) with no live consumer left pointing at it (repo grep: only ADR-0008, the ADR-0006 Status line, the README index row, and three historical records). All four required fields are present, the title is 68 characters, and ADR-0006 was touched on its Status line only, following the ADR-0004/0005 and ADR-0006/0007 partial-supersession precedent. The ADR falls short as a *record*, which is what an ADR is for: its one session-log Source (§ 8 of `2026-10-22_orientation-and-record-backlog.md`) records the profile build and approval but none of the three decisions, the style-model build, the whole-document analysis or the owner's `avoid_vocab` remark, and the session log is unmodified in the working tree, so "Full context" is asserted with no document holding the reasoning. Four smaller precision gaps: the `avoid_vocab` supersession is attributed to ADR-0006, which never decided that field; the residual precedence for the style model's non-Argument sections (Openings, Transitions, Paragraph structure) is left implicit after the old blanket rule was narrowed to "on voice"; a *model wins* target now has two authoritative texts (the profile's stated target and the model's rules) and no model id; and the "owner's documents are weakest" diagnosis compares a funding proposal, a research summary and an early draft against a published AER paper without the genre caveat the same session log attached to Hedging. Each fix is a few lines. With the High and the Mediums addressed the ADR clears the 80 commit gate.
